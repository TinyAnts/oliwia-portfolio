01 · Hitachi Energy · Collaborative Operations Portal

Redesigning the COP homepage into a role-aware command center

The problem

The homepage had been left behind for years. It was essentially a long directory of links with confusing names, so people would log in and still not know what the portal could do for them or where to go next.

What I did

I owned this one alone, from the first interviews to the shipped design. I watched how users actually move through the portal, tested inside their real flows, then rebuilt the homepage around each person's role and subscriptions, with a few lightweight KPI widgets. Developers were involved early, so nothing got lost in handoff.

02 · Hitachi Energy · Installed Base + Sites

Installed Base: a structure problem, not a screen redesign

The problem

More than six business units had to share one Installed Base view, but each sells different products and uses different words for the same things. There was no common user model to build on, just legacy single-unit pages and a portal-wide rebrand happening at the same time.

What I did

Again end to end on my own. I facilitated the workshops, built proto-personas and ran card sorting, which is how we caught the "Assets overload" risk before anything shipped. The hierarchy I designed turned out flexible enough that new content was added later without a rebuild.

03 · Enterprise energy software · Glow / Optimization

Glow: an AI-powered contingency analysis users can trust

The problem

Power-system engineers needed to run contingency analysis where a physics solver checks part of the cases and AI predicts the rest. The flow crossed several systems, and without careful design it could easily feel like a black box: what is ready, what failed, what happens next.

What I did

This one was mine end to end. I inherited a basic flow from a previous designer and had about three months to turn it into the real thing. I refined the flow, designed the final views for case loading, run analysis and the dashboard, kept iterating on my own, and brought every open question back to our stakeholders instead of guessing.

04 · Okapi:Orbits · ESA-sponsored SaaS

CASCADE: a coordination platform for satellite operators

The problem

When two satellites are on a possible collision course, their operators often can't even reach each other easily, let alone trust the data they receive. Decisions that should take minutes were spread across emails, calls and separate tools.

What I did

I led research and design for the ESA-sponsored MVP. I ran UX workshops, mapped the user flows and a priority matrix, took the wireframes from rough sketches to hi-fi, and then put the concept in front of 11 satellite operators in England, France and Italy in in-depth interviews.

05 · Okapi:Orbits · OKAPI Platform

A space weather widget for safer maneuver decisions

The problem

Before a maneuver, operators were checking several space-weather sources and piecing the picture together themselves. It took time they didn't have, and the data wasn't easy to interpret under pressure.

What I did

I benchmarked how existing platforms present space weather, ran workshops to decide what actually matters, and designed one focused widget. Feedback from aerospace engineers is why Radio Blackouts and Solar Radiation Storms ended up as simple values with a three-day forecast.
